`tcp-fingerprint-firewall` started as one idea: decide whether to answer a connection
from the way its first packets are built, before anything upstream has to care. The
idea held, and it outgrew the prototype.

It is now **ELLIO NullRecon** - a pre-firewall shield that drops reconnaissance at the
edge. Scanners get no reply, and your perimeter never lands in the indexes that mass
exploitation campaigns are built from.

To a scanner, your perimeter is a black hole. Probes go in and nothing comes back.
